# US Freight Logistics and Dispatching Guide

A reference manual for US freight dispatching, logistics coordination, and freight billing operations covered by Stealth Voice Copilot.

## 1. Core Industry Workflows

### 1.1 Load Booking Lifecycle
1. **Search and Negotiation**: Identifying freight on load boards (DAT One, Truckstop) based on deadhead distance, equipment type (Dry Van 53ft, Reefer, Flatbed), and market rate per mile.
2. **Broker Packet and Setup**: Completing carrier profile, W-9, certificate of insurance (COI listing broker as certificate holder), and safety rating verification (FMCSA SAFER system).
3. **Rate Confirmation (Rate Con)**: Reviewing and countersigning the legally binding agreement before dispatching the driver to the shipper.
4. **Driver Dispatch**: Relaying pickup numbers, shipper address, appointment time, dock instructions, and trailer requirements.
5. **Transit Monitoring**: Checking driver location via ELD or tracking links (Macropoint, Project44, Trucker Tools).
6. **Delivery and Proof of Delivery (POD)**: Receiving the signed BOL with clear receiver signatures, timestamps, and condition notes.
7. **Billing and Factoring**: Submitting the rate con, signed POD, and accessorial receipts to the factoring company for immediate funding.

## 2. Key Industry Documents

### 2.1 Rate Confirmation (Rate Con)
The contract between the freight broker and the motor carrier specifying agreed terms. Key fields requiring verification:
* Agreed flat rate or rate per mile.
* Pickup and delivery appointment windows (strict appointments vs first-come-first-served).
* Commodity description, piece count, and gross cargo weight.
* Special instructions (temperature setpoint for reefer loads, pallet exchange, tarps for flatbeds).
* Detention and accessorial terms (standard free time, hourly compensation rate).

### 2.2 Bill of Lading (BOL)
The legal document issued by the shipper acknowledging receipt of cargo for transport.
* Shipper signature confirms carrier took custody.
* Receiver signature on the same document transforms it into the Proof of Delivery (POD).
* Driver must note any discrepancies using standard shipping terms: Shortage, Over, Damaged (OS&D).

### 2.3 Notice of Assignment (NOA)
A legal notice from a factoring company to freight brokers indicating that all invoice payments must be remitted directly to the factor rather than the motor carrier.

## 3. Accessorial Charges and Policies

### 3.1 Detention
* **Standard Industry Policy**: 2 free hours for loading and 2 free hours for unloading.
* **Standard Compensation**: $50.00 to $75.00 per hour billed in 15-minute increments thereafter.
* **Requirements for Payment**:
  * Formal broker notification when 1.5 hours have elapsed at the facility.
  * Signed and stamped in/out times on the Bill of Lading.
  * GPS geolocation logs confirming driver arrival within the appointment window.

### 3.2 Truck Ordered Not Used (TONU)
* **Trigger**: A booked load is cancelled by the broker or shipper after the driver has been dispatched or has arrived at the pickup facility.
* **Standard Fee**: $150.00 to $250.00 depending on distance traveled and equipment type.
* **Requirements for Payment**: Driver must have been moving toward or present at the shipper before formal cancellation notification.

### 3.3 Layover
* **Trigger**: Facility cannot load or unload the truck on the scheduled appointment day, forcing the driver to wait until the subsequent day.
* **Standard Fee**: $150.00 to $300.00 per 24-hour cycle.
* **Requirements for Payment**: Immediate broker notification and updated appointment confirmation.

### 3.4 Lumper Fees
* **Trigger**: Third-party warehouse laborers required to unload palletized goods, common at grocery distribution centers (such as Sysco, US Foods, Walmart).
* **Payment Process**: Driver requests a Comcheck, EFS code, or Fleet One check from the broker, pays the lumper company, and submits the printed receipt with the delivery packet.

## 4. Regulatory Framework: Hours of Service (HOS)

Regulated by the Federal Motor Carrier Safety Administration (FMCSA) for property-carrying commercial drivers:

* **11-Hour Driving Limit**: A driver may drive a maximum of 11 hours after 10 consecutive hours off duty.
* **14-Hour Duty Window**: A driver cannot drive beyond the 14th consecutive hour after coming on duty, following 10 consecutive hours off duty.
* **30-Minute Driving Break**: Drivers must take a 30-minute break after 8 cumulative hours of driving without at least a 30-minute interruption.
* **60/70-Hour Duty Limit**: Maximum of 60 hours on duty in 7 consecutive days, or 70 hours in 8 consecutive days. Reset by 34 or more consecutive hours off duty.
* **Electronic Logging Devices (ELD)**: Mandatory engine-connected hardware (Motive, Samsara) recording duty status: Off Duty, Sleeper Berth, Driving, On Duty Not Driving.

## 5. Standard Interview Scenarios

### Scenario 1: Handling Facility Detention
* **Question**: "What steps do you take when your driver has been waiting at the shipper for two hours?"
* **English Answer**: "I contact the broker at the 90-minute mark to notify them that detention is approaching. Once the two free hours pass, I confirm the hourly rate, ensure the driver gets the BOL stamped with in and out times, and submit the GPS logs with our invoice."
* **Romanian Context**: Anunti brokerul la 1.5 ore, ceri confirmare scrisa pentru $50/ora si stampila pe BOL cu ora exacta de intrare si iesire.

### Scenario 2: Handling a Load Cancellation (TONU)
* **Question**: "How do you handle a situation where a broker cancels a load after the driver arrives?"
* **English Answer**: "I immediately request a revised rate confirmation with a TONU fee of $150 to $250. Then I verify the driver's location logs and begin searching for alternative loads on DAT to minimize deadhead."
* **Romanian Context**: Ceri imediat Rate Con actualizat cu TONU ($150-$250), verifici ca soferul a fost la locatie si ii cauti imediat alta cursa.

### Scenario 3: Discrepancies on the Bill of Lading
* **Question**: "What should a driver do if the cargo count on the pallet does not match the BOL?"
* **English Answer**: "The driver must not sign the clean BOL. They must contact dispatch immediately, count the freight with the shipping clerk, annotate the exact discrepancy on the BOL before signing, and take photos of the loaded cargo."
* **Romanian Context**: Soferul nu semneaza curat, noteaza pe BOL diferenta exacta (bucati lipsa sau deteriorate) si face poze clare.

### Scenario 4: Overweight Axle at the Weigh Station
* **Question**: "Your driver scales the load at a CAT scale and the drive axles are overweight. How do you resolve it?"
* **English Answer**: "If the total gross weight is under 80,000 pounds, I instruct the driver to slide the fifth wheel or trailer tandem pins to balance the axle weight. If the total weight exceeds 80,000 pounds, the driver returns to the shipper for rework."
* **Romanian Context**: Daca greutatea totala e sub 80k lbs, gliseaza rotile din spate (tandemul). Daca depaseste 80,000 lbs in total, se intoarce la incarcator.
